Subject left, context right

Same 3-slot system as v3, only the slots are mirrored. The subject anchor (team crest / player headshot / vs-layout) is now on the LEFT where the eye lands first; the context badge (OVER · BY OV · TOSS · MATCH) moves to the RIGHT where it acts as a tidy right-hand index. Why this ordering might read better: the subject (team / player) is the most emotionally loaded element — leading with it matches how people describe bets out loud ("CSK to hit 10 in the 15th"). The context badge on the right functions like a compact meta-tag, closer to the action buttons.

Three design decisions worth calling out

Decision 1

Player cards keep the team-crest tick — always

You asked whether to include it for generic-player questions. My take: yes, always. IPL is team-affiliated — "Bumrah" and "MI" are effectively a linked identity for fans. The crest tick is 22×22, costs almost no space, and doubles the fan-pull. Compare:

Decision 2

Match-generic questions inherit the matchup logos

"Will it rain?" has no team or player subject, but the contract has a match_id. We can always derive the two teams playing that match and render them on the LEFT as a vs-layout. Card stays identifiable ("this is about today's CSK–RR game"), no empty slot, no extra admin input.

Decision 3

RIGHT badge uses a small vocabulary of 4 tokens

The right slot can take one of four labels. This gives users a second scannable axis (filter by phase) without complicating admin input — admin just picks from a dropdown when creating the contract.

The system in one paragraph

Every contract card is a horizontal 3-slot layout: 60×60 LEFT subject anchor · full question in the middle · 60×60 RIGHT context badge. Sentiment bar and YES/NO buttons sit below. The LEFT slot takes one of 3 variants (single logo · headshot+tick · vs-layout). The RIGHT slot takes one of 4 labels (OVER · BY OV · TOSS · MATCH). That's a fixed grammar of 12 combinations — plenty of range, zero visual chaos in the feed.

What admin picks when creating a contract

  • Phase dropdown: over · by-over · toss · match → drives RIGHT badge
  • Subject dropdown: team · matchup · player · match-generic → drives LEFT slot
  • Team / Player references pull from teams / players tables (with free-text fallback for obscure players)
  • The existing match_id is enough to auto-derive the two teams for vs-layout — no extra input needed

Tables needed

  • teams (id, name, short, primary_colour, logo_path) — seeded with the 10 IPL teams; logos already in /public/logos/
  • players (id, name, team_id, headshot_path) — seeded with marquee players; grows as admin adds
  • contracts gains: phase enum, subject_kind enum, optional team_id, optional opponent_team_id, optional player_id
